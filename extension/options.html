Jobadabra settings

The extension talks only to the Jobadabra app on this Mac. Your details and CV stay in the app.

Connection

Filled in for you when this page opens, and saved with it — press Save only if you type or change them yourself. Keep the Jobadabra app open while you apply.

Filling

Each switch saves the moment you toggle it.

On: Jobadabra doesn't fill a form when the job clearly excludes you (location, work permit, language), and tells you why; you can still choose Fill anyway. Turn it off to always fill, e.g. while testing.
For trying out the filling on real forms without submitting: Claude answers every field and never stops for eligibility. Turn it off for real applications.
Some sites (e.g. Greenhouse) only open their drop-down menus for a real click. With this on, the extension clicks them for you; Chrome shows "Jobadabra started debugging this browser" while it does.
Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable, SuccessFactors, Personio, Teamtailor, Recruitee, softgarden, Umantis, Taleo, iCIMS and BambooHR always work. With this on, Chrome asks once to let Jobadabra run on other sites too, so a tab the app opens there can be filled (a job board that sends you to the employer's own site). It never starts on a page you opened yourself.
Used by "Open in my browser": the app hands over the cookies of that job's site so you land signed in. With this on, Chrome asks once to let Jobadabra set cookies. Off, the page opens and is filled without them. Never passwords.
Privacy notices, "I acknowledge", data-processing consents: ticked after filling (listed on the page so you can read them), and Submit is left ready for you. Jobadabra presses Submit only if you turned on Full automation.

Your details and CV

They live in the Jobadabra app (Settings → Your details for application forms), and the extension asks the app for them each time it fills a form. Change them there.
