## 2/6 Yardstick (#3565)
- **Commit 1 (done):** `.github/workflows/search-personas.yml` inputs `presses` (1|2) and `personas` (JSON list); a by-hand run is never a night (no Admin upload, its own artifact name, no red gate). Measured once: a repeat press reads employer sites ~4 s and places get 0 s (spec, "Two facts"); a second sample after the broker's refusals end. If it holds, that fix is high impact (most real presses are repeats) and does not wait for the pure moves: it is the first behaviour PR, right after this PR's band, measured by the yardstick (the order is the owner's to confirm).
- **Per-item AI replay:** new `src/tape_items.py` (split a numbered-list request into its items; split an answer of one list of numbers or of `{n, …}` rows into per-item parts; rebuild a re-ordered batch). `src/search_tape.py` `_Messages.create` keeps the parts at record (`items.jsonl`) and, with `JOBADABRA_TAPE_ITEMS=1` on a replay, answers a missed batch whose every item is on the tape (`counts['ai_rebuilt']`); one missing item = the batch misses as today. Calls it covers: `ai/title_triage.py` (fit), `ai/place_triage.py` (locations, vague, jobs), `geo.py` (places), `sources/eures.py` (regions), `sources/web_search.py` (keep), `sources/visits.py` (keep). Test: `tests/unit/sources/test_tape_items.py` (split, parts, join; record [A,B,C], replay [C,A] rebuilt with n renumbered; [C,D] misses and raises with `JOBADABRA_TAPE_LIVE=0`); `tests/unit/sources/test_search_tape.py` stays green unchanged.
- **Exhaustive recording:** `tools/persona-search/run.mjs` gains `--ceiling` (the engine's `--budget 0`, `src/daily_args.py`: no AI stop) and `--tape <dir> --tape-mode record`; tapes stored like today's (`tools/persona-search/tape-store.mjs`, release `search-tapes`). A new `workflow_dispatch` workflow `.github/workflows/press-tapes.yml` records the 18 night personas, first + repeat press, on the CI broker (plan path, $0), one job per persona. Re-record when a tape is older than 14 days.
- **Replay and the three numbers:** `tools/press-yardstick/replay.mjs` (one persona's tape, clock on, `budget_tape` off via a new `JOBADABRA_BUDGET_TAPE=0` read in `src/budget_tape.py`, wait scale 0.25, 45 s budget) and `tools/press-yardstick/numbers.py` (coverage = right local jobs ÷ the ceiling's; quality = right local share of scored; speed = seconds to the first right local 70+, median of the 18; judged by `tools/persona-search/judge.py`). Test: `tests/unit/press/test_numbers.py` on a fixed result pair.
- **PR vs main:** `.github/workflows/press-yardstick.yml` on PRs whose files are the press's (`node tools/replay-files.mjs --paths` decides the list), 18 personas × 2 presses × {main, PR} in parallel jobs, no AI and no network; one comment-free summary table. Noise band: 5 replays of main per persona, mean ± 2 sd per number, stored beside the tapes.
- **Proof:** main vs main reads "no change" on all three numbers; the band itself in the PR body.

## Rules for every PR
No weight or share changes before 2/6 has its noise band (the ceiling run comes first). After 6/6, each change of a weight or share is its own behaviour PR with the three numbers, and a drop below the band is an owner decision. First candidates, from the research and the 7 Nov measure: the repeat press's ~4 s of employer sites and no time for places; `pick` vs `relevant` (case E); the ~4 s titles get after the read cut.
