# Final renderer pages coverage audit

**Verdict: the whole desktop cannot yet be called translated.** This bounded renderer slice has confirmed normal-user authored English bypasses. It does not assess the extension or claim a whole-app percentage.

Audited current fetched `origin/main` **63ac25faa78d7038fbb5bf1e63221458b0ef1265** using a read-only export. No repository edits, new PR, test suites, screenshots, live app/data/network sends or AI spend. Static source inspection; reachability was established through actual consumers, not an English-string count. Origin may move after this pinned snapshot.

Scope: Profile, Strategy, Interviews, Reports, Actions/Recent activity, Sessions, their drawers and shared renderer helpers. Excluded Focus/Jobs (#3507 root), wizard/settings/native (other pane), extension, external/OS controls and website. Shared command-palette/page-gate findings below belong to renderer code and need ownership coordination with the other pane rather than duplicate implementation.

## Exclusions / false-positive checks

- Canonical models: survey-model, work-eligibility choices, task/stage identifiers, parser regexes, profile document headings and matching labels must not themselves be localized indiscriminately. Survey rows, permit controls, known stage displays, completion receipts and tips already supply display readers.
- Profile custom headings, CV names/content, employer/job titles, questions/answers, free-form preference/rule text, generated interview/rejection/report narratives, original email subjects and raw exception messages are raw data. Their English appearance is not by itself a gap.
- `gallery*.js/html` is the design-system gallery, not ordinary app navigation; its fictional English examples are excluded. `desktop/demo/*` and seeded demo histories are legacy fixtures, not evidence of normal producer coverage. No fixture translations were requested or changed.
- `pages/app-feedback.js:108,113` has English tester bundle messages, but only inside `prepared.tester`; excluded as developer/tester-only. It is not normal feedback-send copy. Canonical English AI instructions in desktop-ai-words.js are agent/control text, not a missing UI translation by themselves.
- Existing English source catalogs are the fallback by design. Imported t()/registerMessages presence alone is not proof of whole-file coverage.
- Reports' Pipeline funnel uses the same Focus engine payload; that known source is assigned to #3507 root and is excluded from new repair ownership.
